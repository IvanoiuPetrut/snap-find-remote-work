Is snap free to use?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever and includes a public profile, 10 applications per month and our core productivity tools.

Which countries can I work from?

Companies on snap hire in over 90 countries. Each listing shows the regions and time zones the employer accepts.

How do I get paid?

Payments are sent by bank transfer, card or popular wallets in your local currency, usually within two business days.

Are the job listings verified?

Every company goes through a manual review before posting, and listings are re-checked regularly to keep spam out.

Can I cancel my plan at any time?

Absolutely. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel whenever you like, with no hidden fees or long-term contracts.
